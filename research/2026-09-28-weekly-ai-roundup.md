## Findings

### 1. ChatGPT Voice: plugins (connected apps) and voice-started "Work" tasks
- **What happened:** On 23 September 2026 OpenAI added plugin support to ChatGPT's "Live" voice experience on web, iOS and Android. It also brought Voice into **ChatGPT Work**, OpenAI's agent mode (an agent is AI that can carry out multi-step tasks for you, not just answer questions). In Work, you can ask out loud for documents, presentations or spreadsheets, use connected apps or work in a browser. If a task is still running when you end the call, it continues in text.
- **Plain-English explanation:** "Plugins" (also called connected apps) are links you set up between ChatGPT and other services, such as your email, calendar or Slack. Previously you mostly had to type to use them. Now you can just talk.
- **Why it matters:** Hands-free use is a real everyday benefit, for example while cooking or driving (with appropriate safety), or for people who find typing difficult. It also shows ChatGPT moving from "chatbot" to "assistant that does things".
- **Who could benefit:** Busy parents, small-business owners on the move, and people with accessibility needs.
- **Practical example:** "What's in my calendar tomorrow, and draft a reply to Sam saying I can do 3pm." Or, in Work: "Turn my notes from this call into a one-page summary document."
- **UK availability:** No UK restriction was mentioned in the sources found. Not confirmed on an official UK-specific page.
- **Pricing / free option:** Reported (help-centre snippet): Free and Go users can use Voice in Chat with the plugins their plan supports. Voice in Work needs both Voice and Work access. Earlier reporting said Work is available on Pro, Enterprise and Edu, rolling out to Plus and Business, and in the desktop app on every plan including Free. Exact limits per plan are not confirmed.
- **Privacy and data:** Existing app connections and permissions carry over into voice. Speaking to an assistant that can read your email and calendar means it may read those out, or act on them, somewhere others can hear. Readers should check which apps are connected (Settings) and avoid connecting work accounts without their employer's permission. Secondary coverage mentions on-screen approvals before actions; this is not confirmed on an official page.
- **Verification status:** **Partially verified.** The date (23 September) and core features are consistent across 9to5Mac, Unite.AI, tbreak and search-engine summaries of OpenAI's help centre ("ChatGPT Voice" article and release notes). OpenAI's pages could not be opened directly. Plan-by-plan details are not confirmed.
- **Sources (checked 28 September 2026):**
  - OpenAI Help Centre, ChatGPT release notes: https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes (search summary only)
  - OpenAI Help Centre, ChatGPT Voice: https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001274-chatgpt-voice (search summary only)

### 2. GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna in ChatGPT
- **What happened:** On 22 September 2026 OpenAI released two new models (a model is the underlying AI "engine" that produces the answers). GPT-6 Sol is for demanding everyday work. GPT-6 Luna is for quick, high-volume tasks. They build on GPT-6 Astra (released 3 September).
- **Plain-English explanation:** These are faster, cheaper engines built from the same technology as OpenAI's top model. For most people, this means better answers inside ChatGPT's Work mode without paying for the top tier.
- **Why it matters:** OpenAI **claims** GPT-6 Sol makes "about half as many mistakes" as its predecessor. That is the company's claim, not independently established. It still needs checking. Fewer errors would matter most for everyday tasks like summarising documents or drafting.
- **Who could benefit:** Plus and Business subscribers using ChatGPT Work, and free users willing to install the desktop app.
- **Practical example:** A sole trader using ChatGPT Work to turn a messy price list into a tidy quote template.
- **UK availability:** Not stated as restricted. Not confirmed on a UK-specific page.
- **Pricing / free option:** According to OpenAI's announcement (search summary), available "in ChatGPT Work and Codex" for Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu. Free and Go users can use GPT-6 Luna in the **desktop app**. Reports say these models are not yet in standard ChatGPT chat. API (developer) prices were cut by about half. That isn't relevant to most readers.
- **Privacy and data:** No change specific to these models was found. The usual ChatGPT data controls apply (for example, the setting that controls whether chats are used for training).
- **Verification status:** **Partially verified.** The date is confirmed by TechCrunch's URL date (22 September) and multiple reports. Availability wording comes from a search-engine summary of openai.com. The official page could not be opened.
- **Sources (checked 28 September 2026):**
  - OpenAI, "Introducing GPT-6 Sol and Luna": https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-sol-and-luna/ (search summary only)
  - TechCrunch, 22 September 2026: https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/22/openai-launches-gpt-6-sol-and-luna/
  - The New Stack: https://thenewstack.io/openai-gpt-6-sol-luna-release/

### 3. Claude Opus 5.5
- **What happened:** On 22 September 2026 Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5, its most capable generally available model. It is available in the Claude apps on **all plans: Free, Pro, Max, Team and seat-based Enterprise**.
- **Plain-English explanation:** A new version of Claude's top "engine". Anthropic's own emphasis is on clearer writing. It says the model "puts the most important information up front" and is easier to follow.
- **Why it matters:** For ordinary users, clearer and shorter answers are more useful than benchmark scores. Paid subscribers also get higher five-hour usage limits, plus a one-off "rate limit reset" they can save and use when they choose. Anthropic's wording is "a rate limit reset, which you can now save and use whenever you choose." This helps people who run out of messages mid-task.
- **Who could benefit:** Anyone using Claude for writing, summarising or research, including free users.
- **Practical example:** Asking Claude to summarise a long school policy or supplier contract into "the three things I need to know".
- **UK availability:** Claude is available in the UK. The announcement does not list regional restrictions.
- **Pricing / free option:** Available on Free. Paid plans get increased limits. (Developer API price: $4/$20 per million input/output tokens, down from $5/$25. Not relevant to most readers.)
- **Privacy and data:** No model-specific privacy change. The usual Claude data settings apply. Don't paste confidential client, pupil or personal data.
- **Verification status:** **Verified** (Anthropic announcement and Claude help-centre release notes, both opened directly). Claims about writing quality and accuracy are Anthropic's own and customer quotes it chose. They are not independent tests.
- **Sources (checked 28 September 2026):**

### 4. Microsoft's rebuilt Copilot: Home, Code and Autopilot
- **What happened:** On 25 September 2026 Microsoft announced a redesigned Copilot app built around three tabs:
  - **Home** combines Copilot Chat with Microsoft's "Cowork" agent, and has Word, Excel and PowerPoint built in ("Office in Copilot").
  - **Code** lets non-programmers build small apps and tools.
  - **Autopilot** (previously called "Scout") is an always-on agent you give a name, role and goal. It keeps working in the background, for example following up threads in Teams and Outlook.
- **Plain-English explanation:** Microsoft is turning Copilot from a chat box into a digital colleague that can make documents, build simple tools and chase things up for you.
- **Why it matters:** Many UK small businesses and schools already use Microsoft 365, so this is likely to reach them. However, reporting says the basic Chat stays within the per-user licence, while **Cowork, Code and Autopilot will be charged based on usage**. That could make costs less predictable for small organisations.
- **Who could benefit:** Small businesses and organisations on Microsoft 365 business plans. Not aimed at home users right now.
- **Practical example:** An office manager setting up an Autopilot agent to collect weekly updates from staff in Teams and draft a Friday summary.
- **UK availability:** Microsoft's EMEA newsroom covered it. Home and Code start rolling out to the **Frontier programme** (Microsoft's early-access scheme for business customers) "in the coming weeks". Autopilot goes to **private preview** at the end of the month. **Not generally available yet.**
- **Pricing / free option:** Usage-based pricing for the advanced features, reported by CNBC and Fortune. No UK prices were found.
- **Privacy and data:** Autopilot "watching channels" and acting without being prompted raises obvious data-governance questions for businesses and schools: who can see what, and who approves actions. Microsoft says code runs inside the company's own Microsoft 365 environment, governed by IT ("Copilot Managed Runtime"). That is Microsoft's description, not independently checked.
- **Verification status:** **Partially verified.** The date, tab names, rollout wording and Scout→Autopilot rename come from search summaries of the official Microsoft blog, corroborated by CNBC, Fortune and Engadget. The official blog could not be opened. Pricing details come from journalism only.
- **Sources (checked 28 September 2026):**

  - Microsoft Source EMEA: https://news.microsoft.com/source/emea/2026/09/new-microsoft-copilot-brings-home-code-and-autopilot-together/

### 8. ICO becomes the Information Commission (30 September)
- **What happened:** The UK data protection regulator changes its governance on **30 September 2026** under the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025. It moves from a single Information Commissioner to a Commission with a board, a non-executive chair and a separate chief executive.
- **Factual information (official, via search summary):** The organisation "will continue to be known as the ICO". It takes over all existing functions. Existing obligations don't change on that date.
- **Why it matters:** Mainly a name and structure change. Worth knowing so readers aren't confused by "Information Commission" in guidance. The ICO's 2026/27 plans include an AI code of practice and dedicated guidance on agentic AI. Its technology guidance plan says a public consultation on agentic AI guidance was "due to launch September 2026". **I could not confirm whether it has launched.**
- **Who could benefit:** Schools, small businesses, anyone responsible for data protection.
- **My interpretation:** No action is needed for most organisations. It's a useful hook for a reminder about data protection when using AI tools.
- **Verification status:** **Partially verified.** Based on search summaries of two ico.org.uk news pages. The site could not be opened directly, and publication dates of those pages were not confirmed.
- **Sources (checked 28 September 2026):**
  - ICO, "ICO governance changes confirmed for 30 September 2026": https://ico.org.uk/about-the-ico/media-centre/news-and-blogs/2026/09/ico-governance-changes-confirmed-for-30-september-2026/
  - ICO, "ICO to become Information Commission on 30 September 2026": https://ico.org.uk/about-the-ico/media-centre/news-and-blogs/2026/09/ico-to-become-information-commission-on-30-september-2026
  - ICO technology guidance plans: https://ico.org.uk/about-the-ico/what-we-do/our-plans-for-new-and-updated-guidance/technology/
- **Potential Everyday AI use:** A brief news-explained line, and a hook for business and school data-protection content. Watch for the agentic AI consultation.

### 10. Slightly older, still rolling out: the merged Copilot app on Windows/Mac, and Gemini replacing Google Assistant
- **Merged Copilot app:** Microsoft is merging the consumer Copilot app and the Microsoft 365 Copilot app into one app. The rollout started mid-August on mobile and web, and **mid-September on Windows and Mac**. You can sign in with a personal account, a work/school account or both. Microsoft says they stay separate and data doesn't flow between them. The web address moves to copilot.cloud.microsoft.   - Verification: **Partially verified** (search summaries of Microsoft Support and Microsoft Learn pages, plus Windows Central; the official pages could not be opened).

- **Google Assistant → Gemini:** Google began removing Google Assistant from Android phones and tablets, Wear OS watches, compatible earbuds and Android Auto from **4 September 2026**. It rolls out over several weeks, so devices switch on different days, and reports say you can't switch back. Cars with Google built-in keep Assistant for now.
  - Why it matters: people may notice their phone assistant behaving differently this week without knowing why.
  - UK-specific timing was **not confirmed**.
  - Verification: **Partially verified** (9to5Google and multiple outlets; Google's own page not opened).
  - Source: https://9to5google.com/2026/08/04/google-assistant-september-2026-shutdown/
